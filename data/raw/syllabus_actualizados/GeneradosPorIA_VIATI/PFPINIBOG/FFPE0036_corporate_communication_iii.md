## 3. Descripción de la Unidad de Estudio

Students will develop the competency to identify and analyze breakdowns or conflicts in internal and external organizational communications. They will be prepared to propose corporate public relations strategies aligned with the communication plans of the enterprise. Emphasis will be placed on the analysis of successful public relations and internal communication campaigns, determining the factors of their success. Case studies will focus on internal communications, corporate reputation, the social responsibility of the businessman, and community relations. (Presente en planes: PFPICDBOG001 · PFPINIBOG001)

---

## 4. Resultado de Aprendizaje de la Unidad

Determines communication plans and public relations campaigns that strengthen the organization against detected breakdowns, based on a diagnostic analysis of organizational communication.

---

## 5. Contenidos de la Unidad de Estudio

### Por módulo (4.5 semanas)
* Introduction to administrative communication and public relations in the industrial enterprise.
* Internal communication channels: corporate bulletins, physical notice boards, and employee general assemblies.

### Por ciclo (9 semanas)
* Diagnosis of communication breakdowns and industrial relations conflicts.
* Planning public relations campaigns for external publics: shareholders, local community, and press.

### Por semestre (18 semanas)
* The social responsibility of the businessman and community relations.
* Case studies in corporate reputation and crisis management.
* Design of a comprehensive corporate communication plan to address organizational conflicts.
