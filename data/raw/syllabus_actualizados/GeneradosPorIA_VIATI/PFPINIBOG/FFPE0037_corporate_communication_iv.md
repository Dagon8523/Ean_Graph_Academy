## 3. Descripción de la Unidad de Estudio

Through this study unit, students will be able to determine, set up and put into action a specific corporate communication strategy to solve an enterprise's internal or external operational performance. Students will develop their competency to identify or prevent possible breakdowns in organizational communication channels, utilizing classical administrative theories and media systems available in modern office environments (such as internal mail systems, telegraphic dispatches, and press relations). (Presente en planes: PFPICDBOG001 · PFPINIBOG001)

---

## 4. Resultado de Aprendizaje de la Unidad

States and implements a communication strategy that contributes to the organizational growth, internal coordination, and public relations of a specific industrial or commercial enterprise.

---

## 5. Contenidos de la Unidad de Estudio

### Por módulo (4.5 semanas)
* Foundations of Industrial Public Relations and Corporate Image.
* The Mathematical Theory of Communication (Shannon & Weaver) applied to factory and office workflows.

### Por ciclo (9 semanas)
* Internal Communication Channels: Design of memos, organizational bulletins, employee news sheets, and suggestion boxes.
* External Corporate Communication: Writing press releases, managing media relations (press, radio, and cinema), and community relations.

### Por semestre (18 semanas)
* Direction and flow of formal communication (upward, downward, and horizontal) in large-scale industrial structures.
* Crisis communication and prestige preservation in public and private enterprises.
* Design and execution of a comprehensive public relations campaign.
* Feedback and assessment techniques: Attitude surveys and physical opinion polling.

## 7. Bibliografía

### Obligatoria
