## 3. Descripción de la Unidad de Estudio

Understanding the power of Marketing Communications is key to performing successfully in modern market environments and engaging with internal and external customers. In 1967, systems analysts and marketing planners must get familiar with the concepts of the Marketing Mix (product, price, place, promotion), consumer segmentation profiles, and purchasing behavior sequences in order to design, execute, and measure plans that align with the company's main strategy. Data processing through mainframe systems is a main asset in this era, and those industrial organizations which are able to gather, tabulate, and analyze market data become more competitive and successful. (Presente en planes: PFPICDBOG001 · PFPINIBOG001)

## 4. Resultado de Aprendizaje de la Unidad

Designs brand strategies supported by digital data processing and market research in order to achieve marketing goals.

*Criterios de Evaluación:*
- Understands the principles of strategic thinking and its application in the organizational world.
- Produces research in order to find marketing insights.
- Creates targeted contents for segmented audiences.
- Identifies measures, metrics and key performance indicators (such as tabulation metrics, sales quotas, and advertising reach) that explain a brand strategy's development.
