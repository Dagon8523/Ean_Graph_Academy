## 3. Descripción de la Unidad de Estudio

This unit focuses on the commercialization of services through electronic commerce, addressing strategies, tools, techniques and the management of the online customer experience. Students will develop a solid understanding of how to promote and offer services on online platforms effectively. (Presente en planes: PFPNEIBOG002)

---

## 4. Resultado de Aprendizaje de la Unidad

Applies information analytical tools through the collection, analysis and evaluation of relevant data on economic and commercial trends in global markets.

## 5. Contenidos de la Unidad de Estudio

### Por módulo (4.5 semanas)
* **Módulo 1: Foundations of E-Business and Hypermedia Environments**
  * Conceptual foundations of computer-mediated environments.
  * E-Business models and value proposition design in digital channels.
* **Módulo 2: Digital Marketing Strategy and Service Commercialization**
  * Electronic commerce strategies for services.
  * Online customer experience management (CX) and social web communities.

### Por ciclo (9 semanas)
* **Ciclo 1: E-Business Strategy and Online Platforms**
  * Introduction to E-Business management and market-space creation.
  * Designing value propositions for digital and service-oriented businesses.
  * Consumer behavior in hypermedia computer-mediated environments.
* **Ciclo 2: Digital Services Marketing and Performance Evaluation**
  * Services marketing integration and customer-focused digital strategies.
  * Social media influence and community rules on the social web.
  * Web analytics and data collection for market trend evaluation.

### Por semestre (18 semanas)
* **Semestre 1: E-Business Strategy and Foundations**
  * Evolution of E-Commerce and E-Business management models.
  * Blue ocean strategy applied to uncontested digital spaces.
  * Analysis of hypermedia computer-mediated environments.
* **Semestre 2: Digital Marketing and Persuasion Techniques**
  * Digital marketing implementation, planning, and practice.
  * The psychology of persuasion applied to online customer experiences.
* **Semestre 3: Services Marketing and Customer Relationship**
  * Integrating customer focus across the digital firm.
  * Customer relationship management (CRM) and online interaction strategies.
* **Semestre 4: Social Media, Analytics, and Trends**
  * Socialnomics and the transformation of digital business.
  * Web metrics, data collection, and evaluation of global commercial trends.
