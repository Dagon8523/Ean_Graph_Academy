## 3. Descripción de la Unidad de Estudio

In close connection with the institutional underlying principles of socio-economic development and entrepreneurship, this course aims at providing ample language learning opportunities whilst exploring possible business opportunities for modern language and economic professionals. The course attempts to articulate the development of linguistic competences – which involves the study of grammar, vocabulary, writing strategies and the development of reading and oral skills, among others, at an advanced-intermediate level (equivalent to modern B1.2+ general proficiency but taught through structural-situational and audio-lingual methods) – with active learning tasks that invite students to reflect upon their immediate realities and future avenues of action as professionals. ## 4. Resultado de Aprendizaje de la Unidad

Demuestra dominio práctico y suficiencia en el idioma inglés en un nivel intermedio-alto en las habilidades de comprensión auditiva, producción oral, comprensión lectora y producción escrita en contextos socioculturales, empresariales y técnicos de carácter formal e informal, dentro de un marco ético-humanístico, investigativo y de desarrollo socioeconómico.

Criterios de Evaluación:
- 1. To use past tenses, future tenses, conditionals, reported speech, and passive voice related to travel, workplace and economic development contexts.
- 2. To read and/or listen to specialized texts in order to identify main, secondary ideas and detailed specific information through print media and audio-oral laboratory tapes.
- 3. To produce argumentative texts in order to give opinions in written formal texts.
- 4. To elaborate opinions about a variety of subjects within their professional field where agreement or disagreement are conveyed using an appropriate range of vocabulary and a mix of simple and complex sentence forms.

## 5. Contenidos de la Unidad de Estudio

### Por módulo (4.5 semanas)
* Becoming acquainted with vocabulary related to travel, job, and economic development.
* Describing and talking about different topics by using the appropriate grammar structures (past tenses, future tenses, conditionals, reported speech, and passive voice) through oral practice in the language laboratory.

### Por ciclo (9 semanas)
* Telling about familiar topics and elaborate concepts in order to state concepts, using an appropriate range of vocabulary and a mix of simple and complex sentence forms.
* Planning and organizing ideas into paragraphs with a logical structure, correct paragraphing and appropriate linking words.

### Por semestre (18 semanas)
* Full consolidation of advanced grammar structures (conditionals, reported speech, and passive voice) for business and technical translation.
* Advanced reading comprehension of specialized economic and organizational texts using printed materials.

## 8. Recursos Tecnológicos

* **Laboratorio de Idiomas**: Consola central de control con magnetófonos de carrete abierto (reel-to-reel), audífonos y micrófonos individuales para prácticas audio-linguales de repetición y corrección fonética.
* **Proyector de diapositivas y filminas**: Para apoyo visual de vocabulario situacional y estructuras gramaticales.
* **Grabadora de cinta portátil**: Para ejercicios de dictado y evaluación oral del docente en el aula de clases.
* **Pizarrón de tiza y cartillas impresas**: Para esquemas gramaticales y diagramación de oraciones.
