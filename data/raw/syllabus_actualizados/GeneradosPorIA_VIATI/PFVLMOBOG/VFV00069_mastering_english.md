## 3. Descripción de la Unidad de Estudio

This subject provides students with several tools required to critically analyze national identities and cultural differences. Today, a critical view about cultural issues is essential for developing multilingual and multicultural competences. Cultural awareness demands new perspectives that can help firms around the globe to determine success and failure. 

Enterprises require the individual ability to understand and adjust to new cultures and cross-cultural scenarios, which is far from easy to acquire. This demands recognition of your own culture strengths, weaknesses, pros, and cons. Also, the ability to recognize symbols and signs from other cultures, while tolerance and openness to different values. Finally, the skill to exhibit your understanding and knowledge of both cultures. (Presente en planes: PFVLMOBOG002 · PFVLMOBOG003)

---

## 4. Resultado de Aprendizaje de la Unidad

Demuestra dominio de manera proficiente del idioma inglés bajo los estándares del MCER con un alcance de B2+ en las habilidades de comprensión auditiva, producción oral, comprensión lectora y producción escrita en contextos socioculturales, organizacionales y tecnológicos de carácter formal e informal dentro de un marco ético-humanístico e investigativo y sostenible.

*Criterios de Evaluación:*
- 1. Student is able to approach and understand the general features of the English culture.
- 2. Student identifies and relates information about culture elements.
- 3. Student is able to compare his own culture with other cultures based on a global approach.
- 4. Produce clear, detailed text (written and spoken) on a wide range of subjects and explain a viewpoint on a topical issue giving the advantages and disadvantages of various options.
- 5. Student identifies and relates information about culture and their different approaches.
